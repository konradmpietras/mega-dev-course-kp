#4471 · split date problems

owner support desk documented reviewed 2026-11-07 · 0 days ago

#4471 merges 412 unique reports about dates, but its source data shows two separate problems. Each needs its own ticket so the work and impact remain clear.

Shared parser reads dates month first

379 reports. The shared parser reads every date as month first. Day-first tenants therefore see dates with the day and month flipped. This is the primary cause currently represented by #4471.

booking-v3 form hint shows the wrong format

33 reports. On booking-v3, the form hint displays MM/DD/YYYY. People enter dates as shown, but the form interprets them using the tenant's day-first format. This is a separate defect that was incorrectly merged into #4471 and did not have its own ticket.

The counts classify the 412 unique report IDs after duplicate export rows are removed. Intake tags are not used because they are browser-derived guesses and can disagree with the tenant and form data.
